Governance

Building IT governance from zero in a remote software consultancy

Problem
Demands arrived by email, chat, meetings and videos. Sprints were created once and never closed, and nobody knew for sure what had been delivered.
Approach
Gradual change in live operations: pilot the method on one project, measure, then replicate. Four phases, each with a plan, a journal and a formal close.
Outcome
One entry channel triaged in or out of contract scope, weekly sprints with their own version, and every delivery communicated formally.

Contract health

Seeing a contract overrun before it happens

Problem
Hours, contracts and tickets lived in separate places. Overruns were discovered after the fact, when the only option left was to absorb the cost.
Approach
A panel on top of the Jira API that compares hours used with time elapsed, forecasts the date each contract runs out, and documents every threshold.
Outcome
Amendment conversations start while there is still room to negotiate, and slow-paced support contracts are flagged as a retention risk.
  • Pacing% used − % time elapsedabove +10 pp is accelerated, below −15 pp is slow
  • Runwayremaining hours ÷ avg. burn of last 3 monthsforecast date the cap is reached
  • SLAbusiness hours, Mon–Fri 8–178 / 24 / 48 / 72 h by priority

Project & portfolio

A portfolio planned against real capacity

Problem
Every project had its own schedule, and nobody could answer the commercial question "can we take on another contract?" without guessing.
Approach
One portfolio view by methodology phase (Immersion to Deploy), with capacity measured from a 12-week delivery average and a 20% buffer kept free.
Outcome
Overrun warnings appear before the deadline, and sales can see the month when committed hours hit the team's ceiling.

Risk & continuity

Contingency plans decided before they are needed

Problem
The indicators showed danger signs, but nobody had decided in advance what to do when they lit up, including the risk of losing a client.
Approach
Each risk gets probability × impact, an early-warning signal tied to a real indicator, prevention actions and a contingency plan with a trigger and an owner.
Outcome
A monthly client health score and plans ready for the first 48 hours, instead of improvising under pressure.
